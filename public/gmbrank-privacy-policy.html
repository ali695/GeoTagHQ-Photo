GMBrank

Privacy policy

Effective 31 October 2026

In short

GMBrank works entirely in your browser. It does not collect, send, sell or share your personal data with its developer or anyone else. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

What GMBrank reads

  • Google Maps pages you open (on Google's own domains): the business listings and search results Google sends to your browser, and the review cards Maps shows when you run a review audit.
  • A business's website, only when you run a website audit or a full audit with the website check, and only after Chrome asks you for access to that site.
  • Google PageSpeed Insights, only if you enter your own API key in Settings.

Requests GMBrank makes itself (deep scan, rank grid, keyword check, full audit) go from your browser to Google, the same way Maps loads results, and only when you start them.

What is stored, and where

  • On this computer only (Chrome extension storage):
    • the listing data of the Maps tabs you use, cleared when you close Chrome
    • "my business"
    • your rank history and keyword-rank history
    • your last 20 full-audit reports
    • your settings, including your optional PageSpeed key
  • Nothing is stored on any server. The developer cannot see any of it.
  • Files you export (JSON, CSV, HTML reports) are saved where you choose.

Business and review information

Reports can contain public information shown on Google Maps, such as business details and reviewer names in reviews. It is processed in your browser to give you the analysis, and it is only kept on your computer.

Permissions

  • Google Maps pages: to read listings and search results and to show the strip under each result.
  • Storage: to keep your business, histories, reports and settings on this computer.
  • Side panel: to show the audit next to Maps.
  • Optional website access: asked for one website at a time, only when you run a website audit.

Removing your data

Open the side panel, go to Settings and click Clear saved data. You can also uninstall GMBrank: Chrome then deletes everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with the extension update and on the Chrome Web Store listing, with a new effective date.
